Purpose

This module manages the complete reservation lifecycle from availability search through check-out, payment, invoicing, cancellation, and guest history.

It is the operational core of the hospitality platform.

1. Booking Philosophy

Critical Architectural Improvement

One change I'd make before writing Django models is to introduce a Product Catalog domain.

Instead of treating only rooms as sellable items, define everything that can be sold as a product.

Product

├── Room Stay

├── Restaurant Meal

├── Spa Service

├── Activity

├── Package

├── Transport

├── Merchandise

└── Gift Voucher

Then:

RoomType becomes a specialized accommodation product.
Add-ons reference products instead of custom tables.
Future restaurant, activity, and package modules reuse the same pricing, tax, inventory, and invoice infrastructure.

This aligns with your long-term goal of building a complete hospitality SaaS, not just a booking engine.
